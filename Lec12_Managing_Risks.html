Quiz part 2 · 1 point

Positive in MXN. Positive in USD?

Bond + IRS: MXN 1,000 invested; six MXN 50 coupons plus MXN 1,000 principal. Local cumulative cash return: +30% over 3 years.

For this FX question, hold all coupons in MXN without reinvestment and convert the accumulated MXN 1,300 at t=3. This is not an annualized return.

USDMXN = MXN per USD. A rise means peso depreciation. With all proceeds converted at t=3, the USD return turns negative if USDMXN rises by more than 30%.

Example: USDMXN rises from 20 to 28. USD invested = 50; final USD = 1,300 / 28 = 46.43; USD return = −7.14%. If coupons are converted on their payment dates, each date’s FX matters instead.
