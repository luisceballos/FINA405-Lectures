Our CFO · Background and goal

Same USD bond. Refinance a peso obligation.

A U.S. CFO finances a Mexican business by issuing USD debt in the U.S. and converting the proceeds to MXN to repay its local debt.
The business receives MXN revenues: how can the CFO eliminate interest-rate and FX uncertainty in the pesos needed to service the USD bond?

The plan

Three steps connect the financing.

  • t=0: borrow USD 1,000; convert at USDMXN 20.
  • IRS: pay 4% fixed; receive SOFR. Debt spread remains 1%.
  • Forwards: arrange today to receive the required USD on each payment date.

The IRS and forwards are arranged today; settlement occurs on the matched future dates.

Two alternatives · Same USD bond

Floating USD → fixed MXN, directly with CCS.

Return to the original USD 1,000 bond: coupon SOFR + 1%, issued at par to refinance MXN 20,000 of debt.

  • IRS + FX: 5% fixed rate in USD; forwards lock known MXN amounts.
  • CCS: receive the floating USD debt payments; pay 8% fixed in MXN.

The CCS is a replacement: no separate IRS or FX forwards.

CCS · What is it?

Two currency legs. One contract.

  • CCS: an agreement to exchange interest payments in two currencies; our contract also exchanges principal.
  • IRS: changes the interest-rate exposure within the same currency.
  • FX forwards: lock currency exchanges for specified future dates.
  • CCS in our case: receives floating USD and pays fixed MXN directly.

The package also needs the initial spot conversion. Our CCS includes the initial principal exchange, periodic interest and principal reversal.

CCS · Contract terms

One CCS: receive floating USD, pay fixed MXN.

Current bond: FV = issue price = USD 1,000 (par). Coupon = SOFR + 1%, initially 5%. Maturity: 2 years; payments every 6 months.

New CCS: same 2-year term and payment dates; USD 1,000 / MXN 20,000 notionals. Company RECEIVES SOFR + 1% in USD and PAYS 8% fixed in MXN. No separate IRS or forwards.

  • t=0 — company: PAYS USD 1,000; RECEIVES MXN 20,000.
  • Each semester — company: RECEIVES the USD bond coupon; PAYS MXN 800.
  • t=2 principal — company: RECEIVES USD 1,000; PAYS MXN 20,000, plus the last interest exchange.
