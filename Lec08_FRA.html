04 · Local YTM

One yield reproduces the issue price

The curve prices the bond first. YTM is the single annual rate that matches that price.

Common teaching example only. Activity 1 uses your assigned face value and coupon. Numerical answers stay hidden until the instructor reveals this example.

Local issue price
Local YTM · annual, semiannual basis

Reproduce in Excel

Copy Excel formula=2*RATE(10,23750000,-P_CLP,950000000)

Price = funding raised today. Face value is the principal promised at maturity. They need not be equal.

YTM = the annualized financing rate implied by the price and all promised payments, assuming payment as scheduled and no issuance fees. A 5% coupon is not a 5% borrowing cost when the issue price differs from par.

The 5-year curve rate is not automatically the YTM. Earlier coupons have different discount rates.

What does spot conversion tell us?

It translates money received today. It does not lock the dollars needed to repay future coupons and principal.

Without FX hedges, each future USD payment depends on the spot rate on that payment date. A USD borrowing YTM therefore requires an assumed or realized FX path.

Prediction: if CLP strengthens after issuance, will the company need more or fewer USD to meet the same CLP payments?

USD YTM:
In Excel: enter a trial annual y, calculate the PV sum, then use Goal Seek: set PV minus issue price to 0 by changing y.

The CLP coupons are constant, but their USD equivalents vary with the forwards. Therefore use the cash-flow sum, not the level-coupon annuity formula. A single USD YTM still reproduces the same USD price. RATE works for the constant CLP coupon; it does not accept the varying USD coupon schedule. If every USD coupon were equal, 2 × RATE would also work.

Different currency YTMs do not rank “cheap” versus “expensive” financing by themselves. Each yield measures payments in a different unit. Forward conversion incorporates the interest-rate differential. Compare financing choices in the same currency, including the hedge; a higher CLP YTM is not a free higher USD return.

Coupon, local YTM and USD YTM answer different questions

CLP coupon

Determines the contractual CLP payments. It is quoted against face value.

Local YTM

The single CLP yield that equates the issue price to the promised CLP payments.

Hedged USD YTM

The single USD yield that equates the dollar proceeds to the forward-hedged USD payments.

A higher local yield does not imply a free extra USD return. The forward rates change the dollar value of each payment.

Part 2 · What is an FRA?

One future loan. A separate rate hedge.

Sierra Copper will borrow USD 1 million in six months, for six months. Today it agrees to a 4% FRA rate.

An FRA exchanges interest-rate compensation, not principal. It does not fund the loan and does not hedge USDCLP. Next class: fair rate and cash settlement.
